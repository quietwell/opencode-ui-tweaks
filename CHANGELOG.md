# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for OpenCode Desktop 2.3.x.

- Session list for the Inbox sidebar with your own sections, a Needs attention group, Recent, and a collapsible Archived section.
- Right-click menus for the list, sections and sessions; drag and drop for sessions and sections.
- Keeps the app on the Inbox layout.
- Buttons under your messages: Revert, Fork and Copy on a sent message; Interrupt and send now, Move to queue, Delete and Copy on a waiting one.
- Jump rail: a line per message you sent at the left edge of the session, with a preview and a click to go there.
- Settings page (Settings › Sidebar), two command palette commands, English and German.
